Welcome to my website!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics in the IDEA Graduate Program at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) and the Barcelona School of Economics (BSE). I am on the 2026–27 Economics Job Market. My advisors are Raül Santaeulàlia-Llopis and André Gröger.

I am a macroeconomist working on economic growth, structural transformation, firm dynamics, and long-run development. My job market paper studies how large-scale manufacturing builds managerial and technical capabilities that subsequently shape the composition and growth of the service economy that follows.

Outside economics, I am interested in film photography and occasionally share my work on Lomography.
