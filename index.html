Welcome to my website!

I am a PhD candidate in economics in the IDEA Graduate Program at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) and the Barcelona School of Economics (BSE). My advisors are Raül Santaeulàlia-Llopis and André Gröger.

My primary research interest is macroeconomics. More specifically, my research focuses on growth, structural change, demography, and long-run development.

Outside economics, I am interested in film photography and occasionally share my work on Lomography.
